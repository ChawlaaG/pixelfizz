Mobile Engineering

Mobile App Development

We engineer high-performance mobile software for iOS and Android using modern cross-platform frameworks, bringing your catalog, service flows, or B2B operations directly to customer screens.

What Problems We Solve for Clients

Does your sales team rely on paper order books, or do your customers complain about your slow, unresponsive web layout on mobile? Relying entirely on web templates means missing out on push notifications, offline syncing capabilities, and hardware features that increase loyalty.

At Pixel Fizz, we design native-performance cross-platform applications. Working with businesses in Vapi, Gujarat, Daman, and Winchester, UK, we specialize in **B2B Ordering Apps** that allow dealers and sales reps to sync catalogs, take orders offline, and receive real-time shipping updates instantly.

Our Technical Capabilities

  • Flutter Cross-Platform Design: Build robust apps for both Apple iOS and Google Android from a single codebase, lowering launch costs and simplifying updates.
  • Offline-First Databases: Integrating SQLite/Hive local databases to store catalogs and cart logic, ensuring the app works perfectly in low-reception industrial zones.
  • B2B Portal Integrations: Hooking mobile apps directly into custom REST APIs, ERPs, and accounting tools to synchronize order pipelines and inventory.
  • Foreground Services & Push Notifications: Keeping users updated with instant order statuses, messaging alerts, and background location services.

Typical Project Scope

We define your app's core user flows, build high-fidelity interactive wireframes, establish secure database integrations, code the front-end layout, handle testing across target physical devices, and upload the builds to the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
