Enterprise Engineering

Custom SaaS & ERP Systems

We design and build secure, scalable cloud software suites—from custom customer relationship managers (CRMs) to complete enterprise resource planning (ERPs) platforms tailored to your operation.

What Problems We Solve for Clients

Are you tired of paying high monthly per-user licensing fees for bulky software like Salesforce or SAP, only to find that 80% of their features are irrelevant to your team? Disconnected tools that do not sync automatically create database errors and double your workload.

At Pixel Fizz, we build custom business suites from scratch, structured to fit your exact processes. Helping manufacturing plants and corporate entities in Vapi, Gujarat, Daman, and Winchester, UK scale, we build light, secure portals that automate invoice generation, inventory counts, employee shifts, and lead allocations.

Our Technical Capabilities

  • Bespoke ERP & CRM Architecture: Designing custom database schemas that mirror your business workflows, with fast lookups and secure user role tables.
  • SaaS Product Engineering: Building multi-tenant cloud architectures featuring secure Stripe subscription managers, customer billing modules, and APIs.
  • Real-Time Analytics & Reports: Writing live data tables, charts, and downloadable CSV/PDF export routines to monitor sales, production, and margins instantly.
  • Highly Secure Servers: Deploying your systems onto private cloud servers (AWS, DigitalOcean) with automated daily database backups and SSL encryption.

Typical Project Scope

We run a discovery audit of your manual team workflows and paperwork, outline a clean schema architecture, code the web dashboard and secure databases, run thorough automated stress-testing, and deliver root-control server keys.
