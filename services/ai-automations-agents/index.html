AI Automations & Intelligent Agents

We design and orchestrate real-time conversational agents, voice-to-voice telephony pipelines (Twilio + LLMs), and automation workflows that streamline support and qualify leads 24/7 with human-like latency.

What Problems We Solve for Clients

Struggling to manage customer support inquiries, qualify leads instantly, or handle inbound calls 24/7? Human workforces are expensive, take time to train, and leave gaps during off-hours, resulting in lost prospects and poor ratings.

At Pixel Fizz, we build custom artificial intelligence systems that automate these tasks seamlessly. Serving businesses across Vapi, Gujarat, Daman, and Winchester, UK, we replace manual operational overhead with fast, accurate LLM middleware agents. We hook into live telephony to handle phone appointments, customer triage, and CRM updates instantly.

Our Technical Capabilities

  • AI Voice Telephony (Twilio integration): Custom WebSocket audio streaming with Deepgram and ElevenLabs to build natural-sounding phone support assistants with under 1.5s latency.
  • Intelligent Intent Funnels: Fine-tuned LLM routing systems (Claude & GPT-4o) trained on your specific documentation to solve complex client support flows without human intervention.
  • Custom Middleware Automations: Writing robust synchronization pipelines to instantly log phone and chat conversations, record client preferences, and log leads inside CRM platforms.
  • E-commerce Chat Agent Logic: AI checkout assistants designed to help clients search products, suggest alternatives, and finalize WhatsApp/Shopify purchases in real time.

Typical Project Scope

We audit your current communication flows, identify the repetitive tasks that eat up your team's time, lay out custom training configurations for the LLMs, construct the secure API integrations, and deliver a fully functional agent system with real-time logging screens.
