Core Development Service

Custom E-commerce Boutique Engineering

We construct headless e-commerce architectures, custom Shopify storefronts, and optimized checkout funnels that load instantly and turn casual traffic into loyal, repeating buyers.

What Problems We Solve for Clients

Standard e-commerce store templates are bloated with unnecessary JavaScript, slow trackers, and heavy themes. This combination slows load times down to 4-7 seconds, causing customers to abandon their shopping carts. Furthermore, out-of-the-box templates rarely reflect your unique brand identity, making it difficult to stand out in crowded online spaces.

We solve these friction points by decoupling the visual frontend from the backend database (headless commerce). By keeping your backend on Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce, and building a custom frontend on Next.js or React, your page loads become instantaneous. This architecture reduces cart drop-off rates and maximizes your average order value (AOV).

Our Technical Capabilities

  • Headless Storefronts: Fast-rendering user experiences using Shopify Hydrogen or Next.js Commerce connected to your Shopify admin via the Storefront API.
  • Optimized Checkout Funnels: Clean, single-page checkouts with secure payment gateway integrations (Stripe, Razorpay, Apple Pay, PayPal).
  • Real-time Inventory Integration: Programmatic synchronization between your e-commerce platform, warehouses, ERPs, and marketing feeds.
  • Tailored Cart Customizations: Custom cart discount scripts, gift-with-purchase logic, and recommendation engines to boost customer AOV.

Typical Project Scope

We review your current sales metrics, identify friction points in your buying flow, and create a design concept in Figma. Following your approval, we write clean, custom HTML/CSS modules, set up the secure API links, connect payment providers, and configure tracking analytics before launching.
