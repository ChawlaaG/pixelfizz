The Core Challenge

The clinic struggled with heavy front-desk telephone congestion, manual diary management for travel vaccines, and an outdated web presence that failed to attract private patients searching for same-day clinical consultations in Hampshire.

Our Technical Solution & Architecture

We engineered an accessible, high-trust healthcare portal featuring an interactive clinical service directory and a real-time appointment scheduler. The booking system handles patient intake safely under strict UK GDPR standards, sending automatic SMS confirmations and calendar reminders to both clinicians and patients.

Results & Business Impact

Within four months, online appointment bookings surged by 280%, phone queue wait times fell by 60%, and the clinic secured top Google local rankings for private clinical and travel immunization keywords in Winchester.
