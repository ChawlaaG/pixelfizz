The Core Challenge

High-volume clinics, retail showrooms, and customer support desks regularly lose after-hours business due to missed calls, voicemail drop-offs, and overwhelmed front-desk staff during peak morning hours.

Our Technical Solution & Architecture

We engineered an ultra-low latency voice pipeline bridging inbound phone networks (Twilio) with real-time Speech-to-Text (Deepgram), intelligent conversational reasoning (Claude LLM), and human-sounding voice synthesis (ElevenLabs). The agent checks live calendar availability, answers complex FAQ inquiries, and confirms bookings via SMS.

Results & Business Impact

Achieved a natural sub-650ms conversational turnaround with an 85% autonomous resolution rate, enabling client businesses to capture dozens of qualified appointments every week after closing hours.
