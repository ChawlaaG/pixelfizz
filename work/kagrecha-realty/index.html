The Core Challenge

Kagrecha's Realty held an extensive catalog of residential, commercial, and industrial properties across Gujarat. Their existing catalog suffered from slow loading times, clunky search filters, and an inquiry flow that forced potential buyers through long email forms, resulting in high bounce rates and lost leads.

Our Technical Solution & Architecture

We architected a bespoke Next.js web application utilizing edge-cached static data combined with client-side reactive filtering. Every property listing is pre-rendered for instant Google indexation and equipped with an automated 1-tap WhatsApp lead button that pre-fills property reference codes, pricing, and location details directly to the sales desk.

Results & Business Impact

The platform achieved a perfect 100/100 Mobile Performance score on Google PageSpeed. Within 90 days of deployment, mobile buyer inquiries increased by 340%, with 82% of all property consultations initiating directly through the automated WhatsApp channel.
